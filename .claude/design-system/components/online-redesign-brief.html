Online — Design Brief

The Online page is the last flagship-tier surface still living as an admin console. My Beasts got Center Stage, the Beastbook got the Specimen Hall, the Skill Tree got its climbable paths — Online got a sweep, never a big idea. This brief is the ground-up rethink: what the page IS, how live + dormant features slot in, and what online play is still missing. Three directions to develop in Claude Design.

Verdict: everything works; nothing is a destination. The sweep gave it discipline (app-blue accent, slab tabs, ring recipe, scroll-law compliance). It never gave it a soul.

2 · The honest critique

  • It's a directory, not a place. The page's big idea is "tabs." It's the only surface in the game whose content is other whole panels — the hub speaks 2026-07 blue, LeaderboardPanel speaks its own dialect, GuildPanel speaks P5-stamp. Three moods, one screen, no hero.
  • The empty-room problem. The home tab's content is other players — and most sessions there aren't any. The most common state is a YOU tile + "You're the only one here." The page's hero depends on strangers showing up; its default state is its weakest. Meanwhile the ONE surface that is always full (leaderboards — Steam-backed, async, populated at 3am) is hidden behind tab two.
  • Nothing happens here. Presence is a snapshot, not a pulse. The network never *does* anything on screen — no discoveries, no trades, no climbs, no guild pushes. Beyond the ring and the icon drift, the page is still.
  • Trading is buried. A live P2P system reduced to a small sidebar action on another player's card. A player who never clicks a stranger never learns trading exists — the system's discoverability is a footnote.
  • Arena has no seat. When Scope B lands in v1.3, PvP re-enters as a fourth tab bolted onto an admin console. The page's own biggest future feature has no reserved chair at the table.
  • Small tells: the detail rail's INFO block reads like a debug dump ("Status: Connected"); blue is trim, not identity (no wave band, no single blue *moment*); the voice strip is a gray utility bar squatting in the hero slot.

Keep (proven, load-bearing): presence-card anatomy + selection-driven detail rail (the roster pattern, decided 2026-04), the imperative root-level ring (the recipe of record), Z/X section cycling, voice folded into presence, the scss banner's accent discipline.

3 · The rails specific to this page

App-color law: ONLINE wears PawPad blue #3F8FE0 — wave band, kicker, seams, chips, focus tints. Gold stays global (LV, milestones), violet stays the one cursor, green stays live/voice semantics. One blue wave bake needed.
Population reality (the design law that outranks taste): design for a population of ONE first, 2–8 sometimes. No backend exists — always-there content must derive from Steam leaderboards or the local save; live feeds are session-scoped RPC broadcasts. A direction whose hero is empty when the player is alone is disqualified at the door.
Copy law: pre-v1.3 battles are AI-mirror (Scope A). The page may tease the arena; it may never claim move-vs-move PvP until Scope B ships.

4 · Direction A — THE CROSSROADS

The Crossroads
RECOMMENDED · ALIVE AT POPULATION ONE

The Online page is a PLACE — the town square where every road in the tamer world meets. Kill the tabs. One full-bleed scene on a baked blue-dusk plaza backdrop. The Center Stage moment is THE NOTICEBOARD — a big corner-cut board of pinned slips that is never empty: the WEEKLY SPOTLIGHT (top climber + your nearest-rival delta, computed from Steam leaderboards — always populated), live network ticker slips (contracts, discoveries, level-ups from tamers in the hub), open TRADE BOARD offers, and the guild's weekly goal. Beside it, THE SQUARE: the presence grid reskinned as tamers standing in the plaza (YOU at front), voice as a small gathering-circle pill, detail rail unchanged on select. Along the bottom, three GATES: the GUILD HALL door (opens the P5 guild plaza as a full takeover — its interior dialect finally makes sense as an interior), the STANDINGS obelisk (leaderboard takeover), and the ARENA GATE — shuttered, marquee dark, your rank diamond mounted on the shutter. In v1.3 the shutter rises and the gate becomes the fourth district; the page's structure never moves.

4 · Direction B — FIGHT NIGHT

Fight Night
ARENA-CENTERED · V1.3-READY BY CONSTRUCTION

The Online page is a broadcast — and the arena is the show. The hero is THE MARQUEE STAGE: a big angled stage slab (signed scene lean, +7°) carrying your rank diamond, W/L record and streak in italic-900 broadcast type. The right column is TONIGHT'S CARD — the leaderboards recast as fight-card standings (№1 headliner plated gold, your row always visible, climbs marked with signed deltas), which means the centerpiece's content is Steam-backed and never empty. Presence becomes the RINGSIDE ROW — a strip of tamer heads along the bottom (click = detail rail slides in); trading and guild live as BACKSTAGE doors. Pre-v1.3 the stage runs "OFF SEASON": standings + record + a dark countdown-style tease. When Scope B ships, matchmaking IS the stage — no restructure, the show just goes live. Risk: the page leads with its most dormant feature; until v1.3 the hero is a very handsome poster, and the copy law (no move-vs-move claims) leaves it deliberately vague about what the show even is.

4 · Direction C — THE CONSTELLATION

The Constellation
HIGHEST WONDER · ABSTRACTION RISK

TamerLink, rendered literally: the network as a night sky. YOU are the center node. Live tamers appear as bright nodes linked to you by thin light-lines (green presence pulse traveling the link); guildmates cluster into a constellation wearing the guild emblem; and the leaderboard top 10 hang above everything as THE FIRMAMENT — fixed bright stars that are always there, alone or not, each selectable into the detail rail. Activity is pulses: a trade completes and light travels between two nodes; a discovery flares a node briefly. Selecting any star = the existing detail rail. Arena = a red star rising on the horizon, "not yet reachable," until v1.3. Engine-wise this is proven grammar — the skill tree already does absolute-positioned nodes; links are thin rotated divs; the living ring wraps the focused node. Risks: it reads abstract where the game's voice is warm; arbitrary node counts need layout math (8 players is beautiful, 2 is sparse, 40 is soup); and it makes the game's SECOND node-map page — the skill tree already owns that visual family.

6 · Recommendation

Direction A — The Crossroads, absorbing B's marquee as the shuttered Arena Gate. It is the only direction whose hero survives population one (the noticeboard runs on leaderboard-derived spotlight + trade board + guild goal — async-fed, never empty), it resolves the three-dialects problem structurally (the hub becomes a scene; GuildPanel and LeaderboardPanel become interiors behind doors — full takeovers, a pattern already shipped for raid encounters), and it reserves the arena's seat so v1.3 is a shutter rising, not a restructure. Fight Night is the better page in a v1.3 world but leads with a poster until then; the Constellation is the most beautiful sketch and the most fragile page. The Crossroads is also the cheapest wonder: one plaza backdrop bake, one blue wave pair, three gate slabs — everything else is reskin of proven anatomy. Pair it with features №1 + №3 (spotlight + trade board) — the redesign and the noticeboard's content ship as one move, or the board is a beautiful empty corkboard.

7 · Questions for the design session

  • Tabs → gates: Guild and Leaderboards become full takeovers behind doors instead of always-visible tabs. Acceptable? (Recommend keeping Z/X as silent shortcuts straight to each district so muscle memory survives.)
  • Does the shuttered Arena Gate ship before v1.3? The current philosophy HIDES dormant features; a shutter on screen ADVERTISES one. Is an "opening soon" marquee a promise you want standing in the plaza — and if yes, what may the copy tease under the no-move-vs-move law?
  • Ghost challenges ruling (feature №7): flip Scope A casual-only behind the gate early, or hold the arena dark until Scope B is real?
  • Ticker etiquette: which events earn a noticeboard slip (contract? discovery? level-up? trade?) and is broadcasting opt-out — where's the line between a lively square and a noisy one?
  • Voice's place: the lobby-voice strip currently squats in the hero slot. In the plaza it shrinks to a gathering-circle pill by the square — prominent enough for a feature you want used?
  • Budget check on every sketch: the square is still a scroll grid (border/bg states only, opaque chrome, in-bounds badges), the ring stays the imperative root-level recipe, and every texture names its bake (plaza backdrop · blue waves · board grain · gate shutter).
