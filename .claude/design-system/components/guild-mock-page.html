Honesty rule holds: unlocked stops lit, future stops dim — and LV 20 (unwired) · LV 25 / 45 (raid-gated) · LV 40 / 50 (copy-only cosmetics) render NOTHING. Five real rows, not ten promises. When a dormant perk gets wired, its row simply appears. Why an overlay, not the chip expanding in place: in-place expansion grows the identity band and shoves the XP bar + both columns on every open; the ladder is 5+ rows — too tall for the band. The centered popup costs zero layout and reuses the shipped modal family (member actions / claim confirm): Space opens, W/S walks (info rows — no press stamp), Q closes.
