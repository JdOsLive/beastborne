Online mock — trade offers

An offer card says everything in two lines: what they WANT, what they'll give FOR it. Click one to trade — the existing live trade window does the rest. Post yours and it stays pinned for 48 hours, even while you're offline. No free text anywhere; everything is picked, so every card reads the same.
