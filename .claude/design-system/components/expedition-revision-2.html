The typewriter — reveal by WORD, not by character. s&box has no text-reveal primitive; the reveal is code-behind writing a prefix of the line every tick. Per-character writes (55 cps) cost 55 label updates a second AND make the last word jump lines mid-word as it grows (greedy wrapping is only prefix-stable at word boundaries). Word-step at 14 words/s (≈ 65 cps at Exo 2's average word) reads as a typewriter at arm's length, never re-wraps, and is 4× fewer writes. Do it as _body.Text = string.Join(' ', words[..n]) on a @ref Label from Tick — an imperative Text set, not StateHasChanged, so BuildHash never churns and nothing else in the HUD re-renders. First SPACE completes the line instantly; second SPACE advances (the universal VN grammar); click anywhere on the band = SPACE. Unrevealed words are NOT drawn faint in-engine (that would need two labels) — the mock shows them at .22 only to explain the stable layout.

Skip = HOLD Q 0.6s, not a tap. A tap on Q means "back" on every other surface (DOC-5 #1); a dialogue has no back, so a Q tap must do nothing but brighten the hint — otherwise a reflexive Q throws away a story beat the player cannot re-read. Skipping a conversation is the irreversible action here, and the house grammar for irreversibles is hold-to-confirm (DOC-5 #14, BbButton danger recipe): a 0.6s hold fills the Q cap's ring, releases on complete, marks every beat of the conversation seen. Mouse: press-and-hold the same chip. The tap-chip alternative is drawn for comparison — it is faster and more familiar, and it is also how a player skips the line they wanted.
