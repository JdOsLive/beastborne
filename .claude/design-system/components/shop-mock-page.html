Shop mock — the page for approval

One screen, to scale (1:2 of 1920×1080). The header wears SHOP over the store's name with the two store chips and the balance on the right. Beneath it: the 30 gold goods as Bag cards in four labelled sections — SUPPLIES (with the Contract Ink balance), BOOSTS, SERVER-WIDE, STORAGE — and one 482px display case that shows the focused good big, lets you set a quantity, prints the price you will actually pay, and commits with one gold BUY. No active-boosts rail, no dropdown, no sort, no signboards, no underplate, no dust. Every name, price and gate is ShopManager.cs; every discount is GetDiscountedPrice.
