Expedition — Redesign Brief

Three zones, one mini, nine requests, one job: "The player is here to pick a zone and go." This brief starts from the ui-editor ledger (2026-09-03) and the audit that followed it. The Expedition page becomes exactly two surfaces — the illustrated WorldMapPanel and the TeamPickerPopup over it — and ExpeditionPanel shrinks to the battle host plus its ZONE CLEARED burst (three unreachable view branches, ~5,500 lines and their loc keys deleted). The map keeps its art and every position and loses its chrome: the SERPINGLIN briefing block, the hover-hint stamp, the 320px hover preview, the red pins with gold labels, the tinted request pins, the teal mini pin, the ambient bob/pulse loops, the landmark tag pill + lore tooltip and the dev drag editor all die; in their place the house header sits ON the illustration in expedition orange with one "N / N CLEARED" chip, the pins become orange diamonds with a gold LV, and the one violet ring visits them. The detail card, the picker and the request popup are re-dialected to the house recipes (r22, kicker = state, one meta line, one gold primary, BACK · Q). Three honest adds arrive. Six open decisions in §3; the mock toggles every one.

1 · Diagnosis

The map is already the right page — the illustration is the hero and every position is baked — but it wears three headers' worth of chrome for one number. Its briefing block spends a 52px SERPINGLIN title (the region, not the town the player is standing in), a kicker stamp and a two-line "2 / 3 ZONES CLEARED" on the one fact the header needs; a second stamp in the corner tells the player to hover landmarks; hovering a zone raises a 320px card with a title, a badge, an image, a RECOMMENDED LEVEL block, three stat chips, a lore paragraph and a "Click to open" hint — a full detail card that appears before you asked and disappears when you move. Clicking then opens the REAL detail card, which repeats all of it plus THREAT pips, a rotated RECOMMENDED stamp, a FIELD BRIEFING kicker and a "6 items" sub-label over two-card drop rows. The pins are red (`rgba(180,45,45)`) on a page whose PawPad tile is orange, their labels gold-chromed, the request pins tinted per zone in red/green/blue so the eye reads three families, the mini pin teal, and everything breathes. EMBARK opens a picker that re-shows the zone hero and lore the player just read, then asks for a team in three 92px slots under a DIFFICULTY row, a PRESETS row with three save buttons, an AUTO-FILL + CLEAR row and a Z X cycle hint — nine controls for "pick three beasts and go". The request popup opens on a placeholder portrait diamond and closes on "Completed 0 times". Meanwhile ExpeditionPanel still carries the retired list/team/result screens behind `false &&` — 5,538 lines the battle host does not need. The fix is not a reskin: it is the illustration with a header ON it, one pin family, one ring, one card that says state · name · one line, one primary per surface, and a picker that trusts the card that opened it.

3 · Open — six decisions, a recommendation each (toggles in the mock)

  • 1 · Scope: the page = WorldMapPanel + TeamPickerPopup; ExpeditionPanel = battle host + ZONE CLEARED (default) vs keep the ExpeditionPanel views. Recommend MAP + PICKER. The "list" and "result" views are behind `false &&` and "team" is reachable only from the retired flow — three branches nobody can open, 2,057 razor + 3,481 scss lines, their own team-select, auto-contract target UI and hard-mode toggle duplicating the picker's. The battle host (currentView == "battle") and the render-keyed ZONE CLEARED burst are the only live code. 1B keeps them "for safety"; the mock is identical either way — the cost is 5,500 lines of drift risk and 22 orphan keys.
  • 2 · Title: WEAVERTON (default) vs EXPEDITIONS. Recommend WEAVERTON. The header names the PLACE the player is standing in — the town whose pasture, wood and mere are the three pins — the way a Bananza-style map names its region. "SERPINGLIN" (shipped) is the continent and appears nowhere else in the player's copy; "EXPEDITIONS" is the app's name and the kicker already says EXPEDITION. When a second town ships the title changes with the map. 2B keeps the app name for players who read headers as menus.
  • 3 · Picker: a modal with the house header over the dimmed map (default) vs a full page. Recommend MODAL. The picker is a step, not a place: the map stays visible behind it (dimmed .62) so Q reads as "back to the map" and the zone pin you chose is still there. Modal = 1320×840 r22, flex-centred on the root (the shipped fix — never translate(−50%)). 3B takes the whole 1920×1080 with the header band, which reads cleaner on the roster but loses the map and makes the picker a fourth page.
  • 4 · Hover preview: CUT (default) vs a name + one meta line. Recommend CUT. The pin already prints the name and the LV; the card one click away prints everything else. A 320px card that appears on hover and repeats the detail card is two cards for one fact, and it fires under the cursor on every pass across the map. 4B keeps a 32px slab beside the pin — "WEAVERMERE · LV 20 · 10 WAVES · BOSS" — for the waves/boss facts; it must stay one line or it becomes the card again.
  • 5 · Colour law: orange pins · gold LV + primaries · one violet ring (default) vs the current red pins + gold chrome. Recommend THE LAW. DOC-2 #1: the page accent IS the PawPad tile — EXPEDITION is #ee5421 and the header, seams, waves, kicker, pins and HARD UNLOCKED all wear it. Gold is reserved for the level numerals (DOC-2 #3: gold = level) and the one primary per surface; violet is the ring and nothing else (the shipped .marker-ring is already violet — keep it, kill its breathe). The red pins read as "destructive" against a red END button one screen later; the gold labels make three colours fight on every pin. 5B is the shipped palette for comparison.
  • 6 · HARD UNLOCKED chip + Q-opens-END (default) vs defer. Recommend SHIP. HardModeUnlocked[id] is saved per zone and the picker already gates HARD on it, but the map never says which zones have it — a cleared pin's second meta line ("HARD UNLOCKED", orange) and the card kicker ("CLEARED · HARD UNLOCKED") are two strings on data that exists. Q at the battle root opening the END confirm is the input grammar (Q backs out of everything, DOC-5 #1) applied to the one screen that ignores it — ConfirmDialog.Show(…, onConfirm: OnEndExpedition) is already wired to the END button; Q routes to the same call. 6B defers both to a later pass.
