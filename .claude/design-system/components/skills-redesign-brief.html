Skills — Redesign Brief

Fifteen talents, fifty-five points, one job: "The player is here to spend a skill point." This brief starts from the ui-editor ledger (lane 0, 2026-09-01), then rebuilds the page as ONE view — five branch pillars beside a single reading rail — using only the data SkillTree.cs already carries. Two views, a legend, three status lines, footers, corner cuts and a five-colour rainbow all die; two honest adds arrive. Reviewed 2026-09-01 — every decision below is ruled (§3); the noun is drawn both ways in the mock.

1 · Diagnosis

The Skills page is dressed for a 200-node passive board and holds fifteen talents. Every gate is zero; the only prerequisite anywhere is "keystone rank 1"; the whole tree is maxable at LV 50. Yet the shipped page spends its pixels on a second STATS view (calculator bar, eye toggle, six category bands), a TREE/STATS sub-band with a keycap legend, a footer under each pillar repeating the pips above it, an inspector that says the branch name a second time, prints RANK 1 / 3 next to three pips that already say it, adds BRANCH TOTALS, and tells you "Ready to invest" while the gold button beneath says INVEST. Five branch accents fight the page accent; corner cuts fight the radius ladder; the reset button has two grammars (click-twice OR hold F). The fix is not a reskin — it is a page that reads the fifteen things it has, big, and gets out of the way of the one gold button.

3 · Decided — the review rulings (2026-09-01)

  • 1 · Every CUT above: yes. One view. The STATS view's job ("what do my bonuses add up to") is answered per-talent by the rail's CURRENT → NEXT band; nothing else is owed.
  • 2 · "Drop the cut edges." No rotated-square corner cuts anywhere on the page — plain rounded slabs on the house ladder: pillars + rail r22, nodes + INVEST r16, delta band + reset r11 (DOC-1 #3).
  • 3 · Node silhouette = rounded square, radius 16 (recommended, accepted). 128×128 at 1920. The living ring is the shared recipe: real bordered child at −10 inset, radius 26, `border: 4px solid #9b6cff * 5` — ONE HDR stroke, no outline halo (the outline read as a second ring live).
  • 4 · Reset = ONE hold grammar for mouse and keyboard. Hold F or hold the mouse button 0.9s; the red fill grows in measured px from the live hold time and drains on early release. No armed state, no "click again", no amber glow.
  • 5 · Page noun: UNSURE → both drawn. The mock toggles A (kicker TAMER / title SKILLS) and B (kicker TAMER TALENTS / title SKILL TREE). This brief recommends A: DOC-3 #8 wants a one-word kicker; "SKILLS" is the PawPad app's own name and the tab the player already knows; "TALENTS" is a second noun for the same thing (DOC-3 #11, one word per idea). B is the shipped copy and costs nothing to keep if preferred — the title box is 38/44 either way.
  • 6 · Zero-SP honesty: yes. "NEED 1 SP" on a button you cannot press is a dead end; "NEXT SP AT LEVEL 19" tells the player what to go do. Same big slab, dimmed (BIG THINGS BIG — a locked primary never shrinks).
  • 7 · Hold-to-reset on the mouse: yes. One fill, one duration, two inputs.

7 · Riders — ship with the redesign, no design needed

  • Copy honesty, two labels: GetEffectLabel prints ShopDiscount as "shop prices" and BossDamageReduction as "boss damage taken", so a benefit renders as "+3% shop prices" / "+3% boss damage taken". Render these two with a leading "−" (or relabel "shop discount" / "boss damage resisted") — the delta band makes the sign legible at 34px, so it must be right.
  • Title string: the title is L("ui.skill.title") — the chosen noun (A or B) lands in en.json/fr.json, not in markup; the kicker gets its own key.
  • Delete with the STATS view: currentTab, R view cycling, the Tab swap, showInactiveStats + the 1Hz boost heartbeat, statsScrollRef and its W/S scroll, the whole .stats-* scss block, `dev_skillspage`'s "stats-all" arg. PlayerStatsSummary stays (other consumers).
  • Delete with the footers/totals: GetBranchMaxPoints / ranksIn / ranksTotal footer math (mastered check stays), investedInBranch + .insp-totals, .sk-branch-foot, .insp-rank-value/sep/max, .insp-chip, .insp-status.ok/.warn (keep one .insp-lock line), .sk-cut ×6, GetBranchAccent's five hues → one page accent + a gold mastered state (GetBranchInk collapses).
  • Ring: already the symmetric recipe (L/T rect−10, W/H rect+20) after the 2026-07-12 centering fix — re-derive for the 128px node (radius 26) and the HDR stroke `#9b6cff * 5`. Drop the `.tab` ring costume (no tabs).
  • Seams: .sk-hseam skewX → border-shape polygon per the migrated .oh-seam (48×12 / 22×12, −3px margins).

8 · Open flag + recommendation

  • Accent — the one thing this brief cannot settle. The ledger's net page shape says "inherits the house violet/cream"; DOC-2 #1 (user ruling 2026-07-12, PAWPAD APP-COLOR RULE) says a page wears its PawPad tile colour, and the SKILLS tile is #FF6BD6 — the shipped page is pink for that reason. Both cannot hold. The mock draws violet as instructed and carries a CHROME toggle (house violet / app pink): only the kicker, seams, waves, section chips and the 2px strip change. Everything else in this brief is accent-neutral. Recommendation: keep DOC-2 #1 (pink) unless the ledger's violet was a deliberate re-ruling — one token either way.
  • Noun — recommend A (TAMER / SKILLS). One-word kicker (DOC-3 #8), the app's own name, no second noun for the same thing. B is drawn and costs nothing.
