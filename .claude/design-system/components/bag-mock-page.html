Bag mock — the page for approval

One screen, to scale (1:2 of 1920×1080). The header wears BAG over BAG with the house FilterBar (CATEGORY + R · CYCLE · SORT + ↑↓ · SEARCH), the gold balance and BACK · Q on the right. Beneath it: the tamer's 29 stacks as the shared item card in five labelled sections — CONSUMABLES · BOOSTS · HELD ITEMS · RELICS (2 / 3 EQUIPPED) · DROPS — and one 482px case that shows the item big, prints its one effect line, commits with one gold primary (USE · EQUIP TO… · EQUIP / UNEQUIP) and sells from a hold button at its foot. No spine, no pouch tabs, no body toolbar, no rarity chips, no hover rings, no relic slab, no empty state. Every name, rarity, effect and price is ItemManager.cs. Ruling 2026-09-02: per-species materials scale the bag, so filtering is a FilterBar in the chrome, not section rows alone.
