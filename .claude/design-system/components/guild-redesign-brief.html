Guild — Redesign Brief

One room, five friends, and finally a reason to open the door. This brief starts from the cuts (the 18-verdict ledger in guild-systems-review.md), then rebuilds the in-guild page as five zones fed almost entirely by data the server already sends and we never rendered. Landing / browse / create keep their bones; the plaza is the redesign target. Reviewed 2026-08-31 — every open decision below is now ruled (§7).

1 · Diagnosis

The Guild page is a statue. Between visits, nothing on it changes but the XP bar — while the three things that DO move daily live elsewhere (weekly goals in QuestPanel, chat in the dock/PawPad) or arrive on every fetch and are thrown away unrendered (the server's activity log, per-member last-seen, per-member weekly contribution). Meanwhile the page spends its pixels on a shrine of 10 mostly-locked perks (4 of them mis-wired or pure copy), a permanent DANGER ZONE strip for once-per-lifetime actions, and a rank tier that reads "Bronze" forever. For a 5-member friend guild the fix isn't more chrome — it's rendering the pulse we already have and deleting everything that isn't the room.

5 · Mechanical fixes rider — ship with the redesign, no design needed

  • Normal-expedition guild XP bug: CompleteExpedition never calls AddGuildXP(20) / IncrementAchievement("expedition") — only the RetryExpedition path does. Wire the normal path.
  • Gold perk copy honesty: Lv5 "Coffer Cut" is dead code; the live behavior is an inline +5% at guild Lv≥2 in ExpeditionManager. Make copy and wiring agree (whichever way — but agree).
  • Never render unimplemented perks: the next-unlock chip may only name perks that actually work (Lv20 beast-XP is unwired; Lv35/40/50 cosmetics are copy-only).
  • QuestPanel guild-goals block — CUT (§7 ruling): THE WEEK owns goals; QuestPanel loses the duplicate render entirely; the hub guild zone keeps its single summary bar and nothing more.
  • Guild leaderboard population: only writes behind the raid gate — deferred WITH raids, not fixed now.

6 · Keyboard + motion — house grammar

  • Keep the shipped W/S continuous flow (sequence in §3 caption) + all five modal handlers; the settings drawer's row cursor + deferred scroll-follow stays. Q = back to hub (the in-guild landing detour dies). No Tab (removed after playtest); no AcceptsFocus.
  • One alive element: the violet ring, imperative, on the focused row/tile — the only perpetual motion. Smash-pop icon hover on the header action tiles (shipped pattern).
  • Entrance: class-toggle transitions, opacity-driven (transforms poison flex widths). No animation-delay ladders — re-renders kill them; if a stagger is ever wanted, keyframe-percentage holds.
  • Scroll-clip laws on roster + log: hover/selected = border + bg only; no transform, no colored shadow; dots/badges in-bounds; opaque band above each list, gap 0; rows as direct children of the scroll child.
  • Restraint: goal CLAIM = Tier 2 (≤300ms pop + sound); guild LEVEL-UP = Tier 3 at most (gold stamp snap on the identity band, one sound) — no setpiece; the log absorbs the history.

7 · Decided — the review rulings (2026-08-31)

  • 1 · Accent: inherited ONLINE BLUE #3F8FE0. The guild page reads as kin to the hub — chrome stays blue, zero new tokens; the guild's own EmblemColor (blended) supplies per-guild personality inside the identity band, capped there. The hub zone's purple dies with the pennant; the teal alternative is dead.
  • 2 · Header: hub anatomy, wearing the GUILD'S NAME as the title. Kicker stays a small "GUILD"; wave band under; SETTINGS + REQUESTS in the right slot. The identity band drops the duplicate name — one fact, one telling.
  • 3 · THE WEEK leads the right column — and it is UNMISSABLE. Display-size label on the zone (the §3 board draws it); nobody should ever hunt for the week again. THE LOG sits under it.
  • 4 · Next-unlock chip lives on the identity band beside the level bar — and (REV 2, superseding "not a stop") it is PRESSABLE: opens the compact PERK LADDER overlay listing every WORKING perk stop (unlocked lit, future dim; unwired/raid-gated/copy-only stops absent), Space opens, Q closes. The chip is therefore a W/S stop.
  • 5 · Goals in ONE place. THE WEEK owns weekly goals + CLAIM. QuestPanel's guild-goals block is CUT (rider, §5); the hub guild zone keeps its single summary bar and nothing more.
  • 6 · Feed v1 ships WITH the redesign; v2 is the follow-up server milestone (server writes gameplay events into the log) — scheduled after, not alongside. The row format is designed now so v2 rows just appear.
