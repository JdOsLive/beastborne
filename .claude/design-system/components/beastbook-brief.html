Beastbook — Design Brief

The Beastbook is the game's naturalist record: every species, discovered or waiting. It already got the 2026-07 structural rebuild (the museum wall) — this brief is about finishing its identity: the page must fully wear its teal, shed the last of the old dialect, and pick a soul. Three directions to develop in Claude Design.

Verdict: the bones are new and good — the skin is half-swept. Keep the three-zone structure and the discovery reveal's shape; the redesign work is identity and finish, not layout surgery.

2 · The honest critique

  • Two dialects on one page. Header, rail and stage speak 2026-07 (teal kicker + seams, italic-900, skew chips). The placard and all five deep views still speak the pre-overhaul roster skin: 10–22px rounded radii, zero corner-cut cards, violet remnants everywhere (#8b5cf6 pill fills, #c4b5fd diamond icons and category text — scss :1731 / :2130 / :2616 / :3195).
  • Element language is pre-token. Move diamonds wear old flat per-panel hues + colored 0 0 12px glows (scss :2556–2565); element pills are single-hex floods. The settled system is the two-tone fill+rim badge — and glows on solids are off-dialect entirely.
  • Teal is trim, not identity. The header is a flat opaque bar while sibling pages wear the wave band. Kicker + seam + strip + halo is a teal sticker; the page should feel dipped.
  • Diamond actions violate P4. Five icons in five arbitrary hues (violet, white, sky, amber, violet) — color as decoration, not meaning.
  • The stage is under-designed. A 460px hero zone carrying only sprite + watermark: generous, but no naturalist framing — no plate, no taxonomy, no scale reference. The empty state is a void.
  • Mastery tiers reuse the rarity ladder colors — one color scale, two meanings.
  • Small tells: the scss banner still declares "#8b5cf6 primary" (the file contradicts itself); the grid deal-in staggers via per-tile inline animation-delay (pre-dialect mechanism).

4 · The settled dialect (designs stay inside it)

  • Corner-cut cards + radius-0 slabs — filled surfaces separated by contrast, not hairline strokes; the Exo2 six-cut type ladder; skew/counter-skew stamps.
  • One cursor — hover selects; the violet liquid ring is the only living selection element; last-input-wins. No second focus vocabulary.
  • Input grammar (game-wide): WASD moves (+ seams walk up into filter bars/tabs) · Space/E confirm · Q back · R is the page's cycle/rotate key wherever R has no bigger job (skill tree rotates TREE/STATS on R; the Beastbook qualifies — design its element/section cycling around R as the SHOWN key) · Z/X are the universal silent cycle pair (reverse direction, and the fallback on pages where R is taken: fusion R = confirm, team picker R = embark) · M phone.
  • Silhouette convention: unseen = brightness(0) black shape + "???" — same grammar as the fusion tile grid. Never leak name, rarity, or number styling on unseen species.
  • Scroll-grid laws in the rail (see ENGINE NOTES): border/bg-only states, in-bounds badges, opaque head-plaque, gap 0.
  • Restraint ladder: tile hover is Tier 1, selection is Tier 2, milestone gold flip is Tier 3, the discovery reveal is the page's only Tier 3+ moment — nothing new may out-shout it.

5 · Direction A — THE SPECIMEN HALL

The Specimen Hall
RECOMMENDED · COMPLETES THE SHIPPED BONES

The museum wall, finished as a museum. The stage sprite gets a baked etched PLATE behind it — hairline engraving rings, a skewed brass-tone registry tag, subtle scale-notch ruler along the plate's base — so the beast reads as a mounted exhibit, not a floating sprite. The placard becomes a true gallery label: a taxonomy line (№ 042 · WIND GENUS · RARE CLASS) in the six-cut ladder, corner-cut slabs replacing every rounded card, two-tone element badges, one teal accent system across all five views. Undiscovered = an empty display case: darker recess, dust-soft silhouette, the FIELD NOTES scrap pinned where the label should be. Teal wave header crowns it.

5 · Direction B — THE LIVING INDEX

The Living Index
HIGHEST WONDER · PERF-GATED

Not an archive — a menagerie. The book is alive: the selected and hovered rail tiles play their idle animations (the animated-icon plumbing already feeds tiles), the stage becomes a HABITAT DIORAMA — a baked element-tinted backdrop slab in the beast's two-tone colors behind the sprite, two or three ambient motes drifting at Tier-1 amplitude — and completion reads as the menagerie filling with motion: every discovery adds a living thing, silhouettes sit conspicuously still between them. Discovery reveal ends with the new tile visibly waking up in the rail. Perf gate: 300+ animated WebPs in one scroll grid is a trap — animate ONLY selected + hovered tiles (static first-frame otherwise), and cap diorama motes at keyframe-percentage staggers.

5 · Direction C — THE FIELD JOURNAL

The Field Journal
WARMEST · FURTHEST FROM SHIPPED CHROME

The tamer's own expedition notebook. The placard becomes a journal PAGE: a paper-dark slab (baked grain, baked tape corners) where the identity block reads like a handwritten heading, lore tiers are dated field-note entries, stats are a sketched margin table, and the undiscovered state is a pinned map scrap — the shipped zone-aware FIELD NOTES hint already IS this beat, promoted to the page's whole personality. Mastery = wax seals deepening in color as tiers unlock. The discovery reveal keeps its exact beat structure but lands differently: the final beat is the page being STAMPED — a skewed REGISTERED ink stamp slamming down with the sprite spring. Most narrative direction; also the biggest departure from the museum chrome the stage/rail just shipped, so it must prove the two moods (index rail vs journal page) can share a screen.

6 · Questions for the design session

  • How teal is too teal? Teal is also Wind's element color and SpD's stat color. Where exactly does page-identity teal stop and semantic teal begin — is the two-tone badge convention enough separation, or do the stat bars need to concede their teal on this page?
  • Do the five deep views survive? Stats / moves / evolution / lore / mastery behind five unlabeled diamonds is a lot of hidden surface. Could R rotate a smaller set of named views (the skill-tree precedent), with evolution promoted onto the stage itself (the chain as small plates beside the exhibit)?
  • What does mastery get instead of rarity's colors? One scale, two meanings today. Wax-seal tones (C), plate-metal tiers (A), or a neutral→teal ramp?
  • Does the discovery reveal escalate by anything? Today it's deterministic and identical for every species (correct, anti-gacha). Is a completion-milestone variant (a bigger beat when a discovery tips 25/50/75/100%) an earned escalation or noise?
  • Budget check on every sketch: the rail is a scroll grid (border/bg states only), the sprite is never filtered, and every new texture is a baked PNG — can each sketch name its bake list?
