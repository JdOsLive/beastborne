Bag — Redesign Brief

Ninety-seven registered items, five kinds, one job: "The player is here to use, equip or sell a thing they own." This brief starts from the ui-editor ledger (lane 0, 2026-09-02) and rebuilds the Bag as the shop's card grid in five labelled sections beside one 482px case that shows the item big, prints its one effect line, commits with one gold primary and sells from a hold button at its foot. The 220px spine, seven pouch tabs, six rarity chips, a search box, three sort chips, per-card hover rings, a relic slab, a rarity pill, a duration line, an EFFECT: kicker, an empty state, a close row and a "locked" hint all die; three honest adds arrive. Not yet ruled — five decisions are open (§3); the mock toggles every one.

1 · Diagnosis

The Bag is a three-column filing cabinet for a bag that, at a mid-game scenario, holds twenty-nine stacks. Its left spine spends 220px on a 52px "FIELD SATCHEL / Bag" header, seven skewed pouch tabs with counts and 1–7 key caps (one of them "Quest", a bucket nothing is ever registered into), a gold chip and a WASD hint. Its toolbar spends a row on a search box, six rarity chips and three sort chips to filter twenty-nine cards that already sort themselves into five labelled sections. Its cards each carry a violet hover ring that competes with the one living ring. Its detail column repeats the rarity as a pill under a card that already wears it as a strip, repeats "8 waves" in a duration line under an effect string that already says "for 8 waves", labels the effect "EFFECT:", and — before you click — shows "Select an item". Below it a relic-loadout slab with three tiles and a flash re-states what the E chips on the cards say. A held item's primary is a sentence: "Equip via Monster Details". Meanwhile the shop just shipped the Bag's own card, tightened (150×168, art 100, name 30, third line 26), and a header the whole game now uses. The fix is not a reskin: it is the shared card, the house header, one case, one primary, one hold-to-sell, and a picker that shows the number the item is about to change.

3 · Open — five decisions, a recommendation each (toggles in the mock)

  • 1 · Categories: KICKER ROWS (default) vs TABS in a sub-band. Recommend KICKER ROWS. Five labelled rows ARE the categories — every kind is visible at once and the grid reads the same in and out of battle. Honesty rider: at the scenario (29 stacks) the grid is 1096 tall in a 950 column (§4) — scrolls ~150px, one S press. A bag scrolls; the case never does. 1B restores a 44px sub-band (the skills recipe) with five rounded tab chips and shows one section at a time — no scroll for any single section, one more state for Q to unwind, and DOC-1 #6 says the sub-band exists only on pages WITH view tabs, so choosing 1B is choosing to have tabs.
  • 2 · Held items: EQUIP TO… in the Bag (default) vs only on My Beasts. Recommend IN THE BAG. Today the Bag's primary for a held item is a sentence ("Equip via Monster Details") — a page whose one job is "act on this item" refusing to act. EQUIP TO… opens the existing beast picker (GetOwnedMonsters), the picker shows what each beast is HOLDING (decision-free: ItemManager.EquipHeldItem already swaps and returns the old item), and the roster's inline picker stays. Two doors, one call. 2B keeps the shipped behaviour and renders the hint as a dimmed slab so it at least stays BIG.
  • 3 · Sell: STEPPER + hold SELL (default) vs hold-per-unit — and sell value on cards OFF (default) vs ON. Recommend STEPPER, value OFF. Twelve Ember Ash today = twelve 0.5-second holds. A − N + stepper (the shop's own qty recipe, clamped 1..qty) beside a "SELL 12 · 300G" hold button sells the stack in one honest gesture; the total is printed before the hold, and the hold is still the commit (a stepper without a hold would make a mis-click sell a relic). Card values OFF: the third line is ×N — the Bag is not a till; the case prints the money the moment you look. 3b ON prints "×12 · 25G" for players who sort by value in their heads.
  • 4 · Search: CUT (default) vs a header-slot field. Recommend CUT. Search at 29 stacks — even at a late-game 60 — is a control for a spreadsheet; the sections + the ring do the finding. Cutting it also deletes the only TextEntry on the page (the @ref-poll, the HasFocus key guard, the "don't steal keys while typing" branch). 4B keeps a 240×32 r11 field in the right slot ahead of the balance (DOC-1 #8: filters live in the header's right slot) — if a late-game bag census says stacks > 80 are common, take it.
  • 5 · Relic loadout: KICKER TEXT + E chips (default) vs three 12px tiles on the RELICS row. Recommend TEXT. "2 / 3 EQUIPPED" at the right end of the RELICS kicker says the one number that matters (ItemManager.MaxRelics), and the green E on each equipped card says WHICH — the slab said the same twice with three 84px tiles and a flash. 5B's three 12×12 tiles (rarity-filled when occupied, #131019 when empty) are a glance-cheaper version of the same count for anyone who reads shapes before numbers; they must stay 12px or they become a slab again.

The grid math, shown. Column = 1920 − 80 − 48 − case. The shared card is 150 with a 14 column gap: 9 per row = 1462 (needs a 1474 column → case 318, too small for a 56px primary at pad 24); 8 per row = 1298. So the case takes the surplus — 482, the shop's number, for the shop's reason (DOC-1 #19: surplus into a value, never a void), and the two pages now share the case width too. Height: header 88 + 2, body pad 20 top AND 20 bottom (the ledger's 20/40/20/40) → 950. The shop's column is 930 because the hub's bottom pad is 40; the Bag has no such sibling and the extra 20 buys the SELL row its breathing at the foot.

The case's interior, accounted: 24 + 300 + 16 + 34 + 6 + 24 + 8 + 44 + 16 + 56 = 528 above the ghost zone; 950 − 24 − 44 = 882 is the SELL row's top; 354px between carries the glyph at .05 and nothing else. Putting the destructive act at the far end of the surface from the primary is the point: USE/EQUIP under your thumb, SELL at arm's length, both still BIG.

What does NOT get a zone: a spine · pouch tabs · a filter row · a search box · a sort · a relic slab · a rarity pill · a duration line · an EFFECT: label · an empty state · a close row · a "locked" sentence · a quest section · a legend. Gold appears on exactly: the balance chip and the one primary. Violet appears once: the ring. Red: SELL. Green: E. Orange: IN BATTLE. Tan is everything else that is coloured.

8 · Open flags

  • TAN vs the Earth rim. The Bag's PawPad tile is #d9a054 (PhoneLauncher registry, confirmed) — and BbTokens.cs:64 gives EARTH the same #d9a054 rim. On THIS page the collision is theoretical: no registered item sets TargetElement, so no element badge ever renders in the Bag; the Bag's tan chrome never sits beside an Earth badge here. It is real on the PawPad (an Earth-typed beast tile next to the BAG tile) and on any page that shows both. Recommend: keep the Bag on the tile colour (DOC-2 #1 — the tile, the bloom, the waves are all shipped and baked) and shift EARTH one step in the element pass — the element palette is explicitly OPEN (guiding-star) while the tile is not. Candidate Earth rim: #c98a3a (deeper, more ochre) or #b8762e; the palette lane rules. If the tile must move instead, #e0a85a is one step lighter and wave-tan would need a re-bake.
  • Section order. The code's categoryOrder is Consumable · Relic · HeldItem · Boost · Material. The mock draws CONSUMABLES · BOOSTS · HELD ITEMS · RELICS · DROPS so the two USE kinds sit together, the two EQUIP kinds next, SELL-only last — one act per neighbourhood. Ruling needed before the build; it is one array either way.
  • The scroll. 1096 of 950 at 29 stacks; a late-game bag (say 60 stacks over 8–9 rows) is ~1900. If that is judged too much, 1B (tabs) is the lever — not a smaller card (the shared object) and not a narrower case (the shared 482).
  • Keyboard quantity on the stepper. A/D when the ring is on the stepper (the shop's grammar); `,` `.` are free here too. DOC-5 to confirm the shop's ruling carries.
  • Sell beat. Balance tick (IsGoldFlash) + the card's ×N stepping is the proposal for the ONE beat; the toast survives only for FAILURES (SellItem false, EquipRelic full, UseItem refused). Motion lane to confirm the success path is toast-less.
  • Boss goods with SellPrice 0. XP Orbs, runes, Master Ink cannot be sold (0) — the case shows no SELL row, which is correct, but a player holding 4 XP Orbs sees no way to be rid of them. Items-economy to rule whether boss goods get a token-less gold sell value; the UI needs no change either way.
