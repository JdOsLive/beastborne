Fight Night — Online v2 · Detailed Spec

The ruling is in: the Online page is a broadcast, and the show goes live on day one. Quickplay ghost sets ship with the redesign (one const flip on shipped Scope A code); the ranked season headlines the marquee for v1.3. The v1 flaw — guild and trading demoted to backstage doors — is fixed structurally: every online system gets a camera angle, not a door. The stage is your fight, the card is the standings, ringside is the people, the board is the contracts, the corner is your guild, the crawl is the news. This is the full-fidelity pass: zone by zone, state by state, in px.

1 · Thesis — the lights are on

One line: FIGHT NIGHT with the lights on. v1's honest risk was "the hero is a very handsome poster until v1.3." The amendment kills that risk at the root: quickplay (Scope A ghost sets — real matchmaking, real opponents' squads, AI-piloted mirrors on a shared seed) is live the day the redesign ships. The broadcast metaphor now resolves cleanly:

  • The show is real. The stage CTA queues you into a real Bo3 set tonight. "OFF SEASON" is deleted from the vocabulary — the day-one state is QUICKPLAY · LIVE with a dark ranked strip reading RANKED SEASON — OPENS WITH v1.3.
  • The card never sleeps. Tonight's Card is Steam-leaderboard-backed — full at 3am, full at population one. The page's hero content is async by construction.
  • The broadcast has a news desk. The crawl is server-backed (the guild/gift HTTP API precedent) — trades, contracts, climbs, and guild goals scroll even when the hub is empty. It's the metaphor's connective tissue: a broadcast with a silent lower-third feels off-air.
  • Honesty is a feature. Ghost-set framing ("you fight a mirror of their squad") is worn openly in rules copy and ticker phrasing — it reads as a cool sci-fi conceit, not a disclaimer, and it keeps the copy law intact until Scope B makes the claims true.

7 · Undecided features — deep dives for ruling

7A · The rival pin — "THE GRUDGE" RECOMMEND: SHIP WITH REDESIGN

What the player sees: a 452×96 plate docked at the foot of Tonight's Card (slot shown dashed in §2). Left: the rival's avatar + name + title. Center: three head-to-head deltas computed live — CAUGHT you +214 · WINS them +6 · STAGE you +3 — signed, colored, updated every leaderboard refresh. Right: a small crossed-swords stamp at −3°. When the rival moves on any board, their card row gets a subtle red-tinted edge so you spot them instantly while scrolling.

Interactions: pin from any card row or from a ringside interview rail (a small pin action; R is free on rows? no — R cycles categories, so pin is a click/E action on the row's action strip). Pinned = the plate appears; unpin from the plate. Click the plate → the rival's full leaderboard profile (existing entry-expand). v1.3: the plate grows a CALL OUT action when both are online — the natural seed of ranked grudge matches.

Data + effort: one save-side field (rival SteamId + display name) + reads the Steam leaderboard values already fetched for the card + local save for your side. Zero server. Effort: LO — ~1–1.5 days (plate UI, pin plumbing, delta math, row tint).

What it adds / the honest cost: it's the personal-stakes half of the empty-room fix — at population one the page still contains a relationship, not just a list, and every leaderboard refresh becomes "did I gain on them?" Cost: one more plate of chrome on the card column, and a rival who quits the game leaves a stale pin (mitigate: "last seen on the boards Nd ago" line after 14 idle days). Verdict: ship it with the redesign — it's the cheapest high-warmth feature on the whole page and v1.3's CALL OUT needs it seeded.

7B · The guild showcase wall RECOMMEND: APPROVE, BUT SEQUENCE TO v1.3

What the player sees: inside the guild hall takeover, a SHOWCASE WALL section — each member's pinned favorite beast as a mounted sprite plate (sprite + owner nameplate + LV, the fd register-row material), the guild's collective face. On the main page, the guild corner's reserved peek slot (dashed in §2) shows the three most recent showcase sprites as small silhouettes-into-sprites — a living hint of who your people are. Browsing OTHER guilds in the guild browser shows their wall too, which finally makes choosing a guild mean something beyond a tag.

Interactions: your wall slot edits from your existing favorite-beast picker (same data, no new picker). Hover/E a plate → the member's interview rail. That's deliberately all — it's a wall, not a system.

Data + effort: the favorite species is already broadcast in session profiles, but the wall must show OFFLINE members — so the guild API needs a per-member favoriteSpeciesId field written on change and returned with the roster. Effort: MED — ~2 days (API field + migration, wall UI, peek slot, browser integration). The main-page footprint is small by design; the payoff surface lives inside the takeover.

What it adds / the honest cost: warmth and guild identity at near-zero ongoing cost — but it's a secondary-surface feature competing for the same build window as the board endpoints and the crawl. Verdict: approve the design, ship it in the v1.3/season update — the redesign reserves the peek slot (an empty reserved slot costs nothing and nothing looks missing without it), and the season update lands it alongside the guild-vs-guild season line where it amplifies most.

8 · Keyboard grammar — one cursor, one map

  • WASD / arrows — the one violet cursor walks zones in reading order: stage CTA → SPAR THE AI → ranked strip → board slips → POST CONTRACT → guild corner → ringside heads → card rows → card foot. It renders as the RING on clear-space hosts and as the SLAB CURSOR (border+fill only) inside the scroll lanes (board slips, card rows) — online-motion-spec.html §1. Crossing a zone boundary follows spatial direction (D from the guild corner lands on the nearest card row). The ring recipe is the shipped hub ring, unchanged.
  • Space — the ADVERTISED confirm cap (E stays as the silent alternate — the settled 2026-07-12 grammar): queue up, open negotiation, enter the hall, open interview rail, expand a card row. The gold snap fires only on ENTER THE RING and BEGIN SET (consequential commits — the rationed-gold ruling); everything else keeps the quiet press.
  • Q — back, always: closes interview rail → leaves takeovers (hall / full standings / team-select) → cancels queue (with a small confirm while Searching) → exits to tabs. Registered through GameHUD.PanelHandledBackKey as shipped.
  • R — the page's cycle key: rotates Tonight's Card category (CAUGHT → WINS → STAGE → ARENA…). Shown as a key cap on the card header.
  • Z / X — the universal silent cycle pair: hops the ring between ZONES (stage ⇄ board ⇄ guild ⇄ ringside ⇄ card) — the muscle-memory heir to the old tab cycling. Silent (no cap chrome).
  • M — PawPad, global. Takeovers own the keyboard entirely while active (the shipped arena delegation pattern: hub TickInput bails to the child), and modal popups register in UIModalState in priority order.
