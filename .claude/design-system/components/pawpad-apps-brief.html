PawPad apps — chat · radio · effects · alerts

The four in-phone apps, designed for the 512px screen for the first time. Ports became apps: each wears its accent, carries the OS chrome, and gets the controls the old floating widgets had and the ports lost. The core model: the icon's color expands into the app and becomes its background — launch and identity are one continuous gesture. Device drawn flat (v9 — the lean is retired for readability), authored lean-proof throughout.

1 · The shared app shell

Every app gets: the OS status row (unchanged — apps live UNDER the OS) · an app header: back chip (Q) + the app's own launcher tile miniaturized at 34px (identity travels from grid to app) + accent title + a right utility slot (badge / station chip / CLEAR ALL) · a scroll middle (list = direct children of the scroller) · pinned footer controls. The accent lives in exactly four places: header tile, title, primary action, active/unread marks. Everything else stays graphite #1a1c23 rows on the wallpaper. Violet stays the cursor, gold stays the OS.

Type ladder at phone scale. 10px/600 is the readability floor on the 512px screen; nothing below it. Alignment is by construction — explicit-px boxes, computed margins, sibling stacking. No text-align, no flex-grow, ever (device doctrine). Q backs out to the home grid from every app (Esc too); Slot8 closes the phone outright. In-app taps go through the backdrop hit-router — every control below is annotated with its hit id. Scroll: wheel over the phone scrolls the ACTIVE app's list (build fix: today only chat's list is wired).
The accent bloom — launch transition and background model in one. Tap a tile → its accent blooms from the icon out to fill the screen → the bloom settles into the app's derived background (accent at 8–12% over the dark base; full-strength accent retreats to the header and key controls). Q reverses the bloom back into the tile. Lineage: this is the launcher's original liquid-expansion launch (the Liquid-flag era), retired for full-screen destinations when the accent swipe won — coming home to the in-phone apps at the scale it always wanted. Engine form: pre-mounted circle + bg layer, one-shot class-toggle transitions (~240ms) — never ongoing transforms in the device (see ENGINE NOTES for the opacity-crossfade fallback).
