PawPad apps — chat · radio · effects · alerts

The four in-phone apps, designed for the 512px screen for the first time. Ports became apps: each wears its accent, carries the OS chrome, and gets the controls the old floating widgets had and the ports lost. The core model: the icon's color expands into the app and becomes its background — launch and identity are one continuous gesture. Device drawn flat (v9 — the lean is retired for readability), authored lean-proof throughout.

1 · The shared app shell

Every app gets: the OS status row (unchanged — apps live UNDER the OS; build from .pl-top) · an app header: the shipped violet back chip (‹ — Q is the hotkey, no keycap: v5.1 removed keycaps from the device) + the app's own launcher tile miniaturized at 34px (identity travels from grid to app) + accent title (.pl-av-title, 26/900) + a right utility slot — ONE recipe: a ±3° solid-accent stamp for counts (4 ACTIVE / LAST 50), a solid accent-ink chip for the station · a scroll middle (list = direct children of the scroller) · pinned footer controls. The accent lives in exactly four places: header tile, title, primary action, active/unread marks. Slabs separate by LIGHTNESS, not stroke (vector rule): each app's rows and tracks are one step lighter than its derived bg in the same hue (accent ~18% over #14101F for rows, ~26% for tracks) — chat rows #222a45, radio #31224a, effects #332c22 (yellow at ~14% — it runs hot), alerts #3a1a28. Icon tiles are all 34px r9 neutral #262040; an element/accent color lives in a row's 4px LEFT EDGE, never a tile flood. No hairline borders anywhere in the apps. Violet stays the cursor (and the shipped back chip), gold stays the OS. Radii: 10 chips · 12 rows · 16 buttons · 20 hero.

Type ladder at phone scale. 10px/600 is the readability floor on the 512px screen; nothing below it. Alignment is by construction — explicit-px boxes, computed margins, sibling stacking. No text-align, no flex-grow WIDTH, no margin-left: auto (device doctrine). Scrollers' flex: 1 1 0; min-height: 0 height-grow is the sanctioned exception. Q backs out to the home grid from every app (Esc too); Slot8 closes the phone outright. In-app taps go through the backdrop hit-router — every control below is annotated with its hit id. Apps are tap + hotkey; the living ring is hidden inside them. Press feedback is router-stamped — a one-shot pressed class (~120ms) brightens dark slabs / dims accent controls. :hover/:active never fire in the device. Scroll: wheel over the phone scrolls the ACTIVE app's list (build fix: today only chat's list is wired). Life: after the bloom, list rows CASCADE in (fade + 8px rise, 40ms apart — the shared pl-cas-N ladder, § 6); from then on things move only when they happen. The motion story per app is § 6.
The accent bloom — launch transition and background model in one. Tap a tile → its accent blooms from the icon out to fill the screen → the bloom settles into the app's derived background (accent at 8–12% over the dark base; full-strength accent retreats to the header and key controls). Q reverses the bloom back into the tile. Lineage: this is the launcher's original liquid-expansion launch (the Liquid-flag era), retired for full-screen destinations when the accent swipe won — coming home to the in-phone apps at the scale it always wanted. Engine form: pre-mounted circle + bg layer, one-shot class-toggle transitions (~240ms) — never ongoing transforms in the device. The clip: on the real screen the 54px circle needs ~scale(30) and spills far past the bezel — PRIMARY clip = mask-image rect on .pl-surface (the menu featured-card recipe; squared corners for ~200ms are accepted), FALLBACK = opacity cross-fade of the two pre-sized layers. The screen never gains overflow:hidden. Full layer laws in ENGINE NOTES.

6 · Life — the motion story

Verdict on rev 2: "they don't feel like they have much life." Right — the restraint was written as no motion, when the doctrine only bans ambient motion on surfaces. The fix is not more ambient: it is that every event the app knows about becomes visible motion — a message lands, a boost runs out, a track changes, an alert knocks — and every tap answers. Surfaces stay solid; things ARRIVE, things LEAVE, the one clock TICKS. Layers (guiding-star): SNAP≤150ms, the control you touched · ARRIVEevent-triggered one-shot ≤640ms — the guiding-star's entrance rule, applied per event, not a fourth layer · FLOWself-moving — the visualizer and chat's beast sprites, nothing else · TICKthe 1 Hz clock the timers ride (a value update, not motion — until the last minute). Each app gets ONE hero motion (bold in its table) and the rest is support — Balatro's ambient rule, Persona's "elements arrive in sequence, not as a block." Tier: everything here is Tier 1–2. Nothing celebrates; the phone is where you check, and the peeks outside it are the knock.
