PawPad apps — chat · radio · effects · alerts

The four in-phone apps, designed for the 512px screen for the first time. Ports became apps: each wears its accent, carries the OS chrome, and gets the controls the old floating widgets had and the ports lost. The core model: the icon's color expands into the app and becomes its background — launch and identity are one continuous gesture. Device drawn flat (v9 — the lean is retired for readability), authored lean-proof throughout.

1 · The shared app shell

Every app gets: the OS status row (unchanged — apps live UNDER the OS; build from .pl-top) · an app header: the HOUSE HEADER at phone scale (§ 1b — rev 6), and NOT A BOX — it sits directly on the app's derived background: 10px ONE-WORD accent KICKER (MESSAGES / MUSIC / BOOSTS / ALERTS) → 34px italic-900 CREAM title starting at the margin (.pl-av-title re-sized, house cream) + the two accent SEAM bars (the ONE angled element on the phone) → a 40px WAVE BAND of the app's own PHONE-PERIOD strips at page brightness (.55/.75) as the header's bottom edge — no base, no radius, no seam line. The BACK affordance is OS chrome now: a 28px accent circle with a cream ‹ at the left of the status row (hit-back; Q is the hotkey, no keycap: v5.1 removed keycaps from the device). A right utility slot on the title line only where it MEANS something — ONE recipe, FLAT: RADIO's straight accent-ink station chip, EFFECTS' straight stamp count pill (4 ACTIVE — the launcher badge's pill); chat and alerts carry none ("LAST 50" is gone) · a scroll middle (list = direct children of the scroller) · pinned footer controls. Under all of it, a LAYERED SURFACE (§ 1b): the bloom's tint + the app's own glyph as a 260px ghost watermark low-right + a soft accent corner wash top-left — four rooms, not four tints. The accent lives in exactly five places: the OS back circle, the header (kicker, seams, waves), the primary action, active/unread marks, and the ghost glyph as ink. The rev-3 34px header tile retires — the ghost glyph IS the tile. The phone is FLAT (rev 5): section chips and count stamps are straight pills, the channel bar and launcher tiles already are; skew and tilt belong to the game pages. The only angled thing on the device is the title's seam bars. Slabs separate by LIGHTNESS, not stroke (vector rule): each app's rows and tracks are one step lighter than its derived bg in the same hue (accent ~18% over #14101F for rows, ~26% for tracks) — chat rows #222a45, radio #31224a, effects #332c22 (yellow at ~14% — it runs hot), alerts #3a1a28. Icon tiles are all 34px r9 neutral #262040; an element/accent color lives in a row's 4px LEFT EDGE, never a tile flood. No hairline borders anywhere in the apps. Violet stays the cursor, gold stays the OS (the back circle is the one app-accent element in the OS row — it says which app you are in). Radii: 10 chips · 12 rows · 16 buttons · 20 hero.

The accent bloom — launch transition and background model in one. Tap a tile → its accent blooms from the icon out to fill the screen → the bloom settles into the app's derived background (accent at 8–12% over the dark base; full-strength accent retreats to the header and key controls). Lineage: this is the launcher's original liquid-expansion launch (the Liquid-flag era), retired for full-screen destinations when the accent swipe won — coming home to the in-phone apps at the scale it always wanted. The clip: on the real screen the 54px circle needs ~scale(30) and spills far past the bezel — PRIMARY clip = mask-image rect on .pl-surface (the menu featured-card recipe; squared corners for ~200ms are accepted), FALLBACK = opacity cross-fade of the two pre-sized layers. 6 · Life — the motion story

Verdict on rev 2: "they don't feel like they have much life." Right — the restraint was written as no motion, when the doctrine only bans ambient motion on surfaces. The fix is not more ambient: it is that every event the app knows about becomes visible motion — a message lands, a boost runs out, a track changes, an alert knocks — and every tap answers. Surfaces stay solid; things ARRIVE, things LEAVE, the one clock TICKS. Layers (guiding-star): SNAP≤150ms, the control you touched · ARRIVEevent-triggered one-shot ≤640ms — the guiding-star's entrance rule, applied per event, not a fourth layer · FLOWself-moving — the visualizer and chat's beast sprites, nothing else · TICKthe 1 Hz clock the timers ride (a value update, not motion — until the last minute). Each app gets ONE hero motion (bold in its table) and the rest is support — Balatro's ambient rule, Persona's "elements arrive in sequence, not as a block." Tier: everything here is Tier 1–2. Nothing celebrates; the phone is where you check, and the peeks outside it are the knock.

What stays still. Surfaces are solid: no slab breathes, ripples, drifts or glows; rows never bob (Balatro's float is for living things — here that is the beast sprite in a showcase, and nothing else). No ambient motion anywhere except the radio visualizer (and it stops when the music does) and those sprites. Kickers, stamps, tiles, skill-bonus rows, the OS status row and the timestamps never move. Neither does the room itself (rev 4): the header's waves sit still (drift is a verify-live option, not the design), the ghost glyph and corner wash are paint, not weather. Timers tick, they do not animate — until the last minute. Every other motion in the four apps has a cause you can name in one word: tapped, arrived, expired, cleared, changed. If a motion has no cause, it is ambient, and ambient is banned in the device.
