Shop — Redesign Brief

Thirty gold goods, twenty-three boss-token goods, one job: "The player is here to buy a thing." This brief starts from the ui-editor ledger (lane 0, 2026-09-02) and rebuilds the page as the Bag's card grid in four labelled sections beside one display case that shows the good big, takes a quantity, prints the price you will actually pay and commits with one gold BUY. A boosts rail, a dropdown, a sort, signboards, plinths, an underplate, dust motes, three stamps and a seven-signal purchase all die; three honest adds arrive. Not yet ruled — six decisions are open (§3); the mock toggles every one.

1 · Diagnosis

The Shop is a market-stall diorama wrapped around a very small catalogue. Thirty gold goods already sort themselves into four sections, yet the header spends a category dropdown AND a sort button on them; the page's left edge spends a whole rail on boosts the HUD effects widget, the phone EFFECTS app and the good's own timer already show; a running boost is announced three times on one card (ACTIVE tag, check overlay, shelf tint); the display case rests on a skewed underplate with dust motes, three stamps, a marketing hook, a SAVE X% badge and a "Pick from the counter" empty state — and by default it shows a 300,000-gold storage expansion to a player holding a few thousand. The goods themselves float on transparent plinths with hanging price gems that print the LIST price while the till charges the discounted one — a real bug. Buying fires seven feedback signals. Meanwhile the Bag already owns a solid, proven item card. The fix is not a reskin: it is the Bag's card, the hub's header, one case, one BUY, one beat.

3 · Open — six decisions, a recommendation each (toggles in the mock)

  • 1 · Categories: NONE (section kickers only) vs three FilterBar chips in the right slot. Recommend NONE. Four kicker rows ARE the categories; a filter state is one more thing to reset with Q. Honesty rider: at Bag-card size the gold grid is 1240px tall in a 930 column (§4) — it scrolls about a third of a screen, one S press per row. If that scroll is judged too much, 1B (SUPPLIES · BOOSTS · STORAGE chips, 32 r11, house header-chip family) makes any single section fit without scrolling; the boss store never needs them (872 fits).
  • 2 · Goods as CARDS vs ROWS. Recommend CARDS. DOC-6 #10: a list of things WITH ART is a card grid; full-width rows strand the art from the action. Rows also don't buy the one-screen (30 × 40 + kickers = 1,400+). The card is the Bag's object — one recipe, two pages.
  • 3 · Quantity: − + stepper only vs stepper + MAX. Recommend − + only. The goods that stack are ink packs and boosts; MAX on a 2-hour Tamer XP Boost means "spend everything on this" — a footgun dressed as a convenience. The stepper clamps at floor(gold ÷ price) anyway (shipped GetMaxQty). No "unit / units" either way — the numeral stands alone.
  • 4 · Discount: baked into every price (struck list ONLY on the spotlight) vs a "−9% BARGAIN HUNTER" line. Recommend BAKED. One number per good, and it is the number the till charges (ADD 3 fixes the tag bug the same way). The spotlight keeps its struck list price because a sale is a story worth one extra number; a permanent talent discount is not — it shows up as "shop prices" on the Skills page already. 4B prints list prices on tags and adds the line under the case price; it costs a struck price on every case.
  • 5 · Case on open: the first AFFORDABLE good vs the spotlight. Recommend FIRST AFFORDABLE. Today's SetSpotlight ladder lands on Storage Expansion (+100) — 300,000 gold, LV 20 — so a new player's first sight of the shop is a thing they cannot buy for weeks under a "need +296,000" line. The spotlight still lives on its own card with the 15% OFF TODAY stamp; the case auto-selects the first good the player can actually afford, so the ring and BUY always have a live target (NO NOTHING — DOC-1 #10). 5B keeps the shipped behaviour.
  • 6 · Page accent: A = AMBER chrome (#e0a030 kicker · seams · waves · chips · strip) with BUY in the primary gold, vs B = gold chrome everywhere. Recommend A. DOC-2 #1 says a page wears its PawPad tile colour and the SHOP tile is gold #ffce3a — but DOC-2 #3 says gold is the PRIMARY: one BUY per view, plus coins/balance/price because currency IS a gold role. On B the kicker, seams, waves, four section chips, the active store chip AND the BUY are all the same gold; the primary stops being special on the one page where the primary is a purchase. A keeps the page unmistakably warm and "the gold page" at a glance (amber is gold's bronze shadow — same hue family, one step darker/desaturated) while gold itself is rationed to the things you spend and press. Cost: one new wave bake (wave-amber-a/b.png) and a PawPad tile that is #ffce3a while the page chrome is #e0a030 — flag for the audit lane; if the tile rule is held strictly, B is one token swap.

The grid math, shown. Column = 1920 − 80 − 48 − case. The Bag card is 150 with a 14 column gap: 9 per row = 1462 (needs a 1474 column → case 318, too small for a 56px BUY at pad 24); 8 per row = 1298. Leaving 1298 inside the ledger's 1420 column strands 122px of void beside the grid (centring the rows misaligns the kicker rows from the header's margin; a 30px gap is a different grid from the Bag's). Widening the card to 162 (8 × 162 + 7 × 14 = 1394) makes it a different object — the MERGE says the SAME object. So the surplus goes to the case: 372 → 482 (DOC-1 #19: surplus into a value, never a void). The case is the transaction surface and the page's hero; 434 of inner width buys a 300-tall art slab and a 434×56 BUY. Height: the hub header is 88 + 2, not the skills page's 110; body pad 20/40/40/40 (the hub's own bottom 40) lands the 930 column.

What does NOT get a zone: a boosts rail · a dropdown or sort · a sub-band · a legend · a "More stock soon" card · an empty-case state · an underplate, dust, hook line, SAVE badge, met chip, LV chip, count badges · a sell flow. Gold appears on exactly: BUY, coin glyphs, the balance chip, the effective price. Violet appears once: the ring. Amber is everything else that is coloured.

8 · Open flags

  • Accent vs tile rule (decision 6). DOC-2 #1 (a page wears its PawPad tile colour, #ffce3a) collides with DOC-2 #3 (gold = the one primary) on this page alone. Recommend A (amber chrome, gold BUY) and record the exception in DOC-2; if the tile rule wins, B is one token swap and the wave bake is the shipped gold one. The PawPad tile stays #ffce3a either way.
  • The one-screen claim. The ledger's "30 items in 4 labelled sections fit one screen" is false at the Bag's card size (1240 of 930). The dropdown cut still stands on its own (kickers are the categories); if the ~310px scroll is unwanted, decision 1B (three chips) or a smaller card (a different object) are the only levers — the case cannot shrink below 372 without the BUY losing its 56.
  • Keyboard quantity. − / + are mouse stops; no key is proposed here. `[ ]` are reserved (radio skip), `,` `.` are freed by the A/D swap and could take − / + — DOC-5 (input lane) to rule; the mock's key legend marks it OPEN.
  • Case width 482 vs the 372 rail elsewhere. The Skills rail is 372 because five 268 pillars fill the left; the shop's left is a fixed-card grid whose row width is quantised — 482 is what the math leaves. If cross-page consistency is preferred over the void, the fallback is the ledger's 372 with the 122px slack placed between the grid and the case (a 170 gap) — drawn nowhere, not recommended.
  • Boss-store MAX HELD copy. Master Ink "limit 1" at 1 held: card prints MAX · LIMIT 1, case BUY reads MAX HELD dimmed. Confirm against IsConsumableMaxed / GetConsumableMaxStack (ItemManager MaxStack, default 99) before wiring.
