Quests — redesign brief · final

"The player is here to claim what they have earned and see what is left to do." This is the brief the build lanes receive. It is the fewest-controls angle — 7 opens the page with the ring already seated on the first thing you can claim, Space claims it, the ring glides to the next; N claims cost N+1 presses — with the ten judge grafts folded in: the waves re-baked at the real tile green, the Shop commit beat instead of an unmounting button, the daily/weekly bonus kept as a CONTROL in the band (the auto-grant is a reward-rules change and is parked as a user question), a badge that counts only what this page can clear, gold rationed to "yours to take", honest available/locked side rows, the Guild gold-ink DONE, a dev hook that needs no manager setters, a motion table that names every beat's layer, and the 72px streak strip drawn as the drop-in if the LOGIN hero reads oversized live. Five tabs, five theater kickers, a 7-tile calendar, two bonus slabs, pins, tilts, diamond gem tags, a category rainbow, three side-quest sections, four empty notes, seven ambient loops and a "Chapter 1 — Coming Soon" all die. What remains: one green header, two columns, five filled slabs, one row recipe, one hero, and five things you can press — CLAIM · CLAIM BONUS · ACCEPT · BACK·Q, and nothing else.

1 · The angle, and what the synthesis changed

Four verbs a player needs here — claim today's login, claim finished missions (and the bonus for finishing them all), accept a side quest, leave. Everything else the shipped page draws is the same fact told twice (READY counts on the PawPad badge, five tab badges and the gold buttons), a stat nobody acts on ("N total" logins), a feature that does not exist (the main story), or chrome pretending to be paper. So: the cursor visits only pressables; rows you cannot act on are read, not walked. There is no scroll — the content is fixed-count (login 1 · daily 5 · weekly 3 · monthly 1 · side 3 definitions) and fits 1920×1080 by column math, so the scroll-clip law family never applies. R / Z / X have no job. Grafted from the other two briefs: the bonus is a control again (a 30 r11 gold CLAIM BONUS chip in the band — the second ring costume is its price); the CLAIM button stays mounted through a 0.5s white → gold → green CLAIMED ✓ window (the Shop beat) while the ring has already moved on; the PawPad badge stops counting milestones the page never draws; reward numerals are cream at rest and gold only when a press pays; an available side row draws no bar; DONE is the Guild's gold-ink darkchip; the dev hook uses the public MissionState setters; the header waves are baked at #3fb45e (wave-green is #4ADE80, the old expedition green). One correction of the synthesis' own: QuestPanel is SideQuestManager's only UI consumer, so the side-quest lore descriptions (98–104 characters) have nowhere else to live — the row's description lane is a two-line 13/16 box in both row stops; the column math is unchanged.
