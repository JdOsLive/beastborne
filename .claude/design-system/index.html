BEASTBORNE UI

North star: make the deep feel simple. Beastborne is genuinely complex — genetics, breeding, fusion, teams — and the UI's whole job is to make that feel approachable, warm, obvious at a glance. The beasts are the heroes; UI is the frame. Dark violet surfaces, Persona-adjacent skews and counter-skews, italic-900 display type, filled slabs separated by contrast instead of hairline strokes, one saturated accent per view, one perpetually-alive violet cursor, and motion that spends its weight where the consequence is.

The signature — the liquid violet cursor

The liquid violet cursor is the one living element on every surface — hover and selection are the same organism. A real bordered ring (#9B6CFF, host radius +10) that undulates on an imperative multi-sine wave, liquid-stretches toward its destination on every hop (0.27s glide), recolors pink in the Gene Forge, and snaps gold at the moment of GO. Everything else stays solid so the ring reads alive. The gold snap is rationed: it fires only on consequential commits — PLAY, fuse confirm, release, contract signing. Everyday activations keep the quiet press-down. Gold spent freely is gold worth nothing. → full anatomy

Principles (the gut-check)

P1 — Simple surface, deep systemLead with one action + the few numbers that matter; tuck genetics and edge stats behind progressive reveal. First read ≠ spreadsheet.
P2 — Readable at a glanceBig type, high contrast, generous space. Parse the most important thing in under a second — if two things fight for "most important," one is wrong.
P3 — Beasts are the heroesChrome stays dark and quiet so sprites pop. A panel never out-shouts a beast; element color lives in badges, never card floods.
P4 — Color carries meaningNever decorate with color. One accent per view; the semantic accents keep fixed meanings everywhere.
P5 — Playful, not noisyChunky pills, skewed stamps, italic energy, dry wit — discipline underneath. Restraint on the routine is what lets the big moments land.
P6 — The next step is always obviousTeach through the interface. One hero per screen; consequence = weight: FAST 0.12s acks, 0.27s glide for the cursor, setpieces reserved for real milestones. Earned, never gacha-shaped.
