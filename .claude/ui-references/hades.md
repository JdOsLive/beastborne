# Reference: Hades (+ Hades II)

**Axis:** Impact (reveal sequences, reward pacing, earned moments)
**Why it matters:** Hades is the best in the industry at making a single reward feel like a gift. If a Beastborne moment needs *weight* — the Day 7 legendary, a milestone unlock, a rare drop — Hades is the first reference to open.

## Techniques to steal

### The 3-stage boon reveal
When Zagreus picks up a boon, it reveals in a deliberate sequence:

1. **Silhouette/card enters with anticipation** — slight zoom-in, flash border, distinct "showing up" sound
2. **Name and rarity slam on** — text lands hard with a secondary sound, color-coded by rarity
3. **Description fades in** — body text gently appears after the name has held for a moment

Each stage has a distinct sound. The whole sequence is ~1-2 seconds. You cannot skip past the anticipation — the game makes you feel the *pause* before the reveal.

**Apply to Beastborne:**
- **Day 7 legendary reveal** — this is the perfect template. Silhouette resolves → flash → name slam ("LEGENDARY: [monster name]") → description/stats fade in → claim button appears. ~4-6 second total sequence.
- **Milestone unlocks** (total login day milestones) — smaller version of the same pattern. Silhouette of what's unlocking → name → reward summary.

### Earned language vs lucky language
Hades never says "you got lucky" or "rare drop!" — it says "Athena grants you her favor." The framing is always about the *giver* and the *earning*. Even when RNG is heavy, the player feels chosen, not rolled.

**Apply to Beastborne:** when claiming rewards, framing matters. "TODAY'S REWARD" (what you have now) is good. "REWARD UNLOCKED" (what changed) is good. Never "YOU GOT LUCKY!" or "RARE DROP!" — these are gacha framings and violate the anti-gacha guardrail.

### Portrait reactions
Zagreus's portrait in the corner reacts to what's happening — different expressions for different boon gods, winks at particularly good picks. It's not just decorative; it's *reaction feedback* that makes the player feel seen.

**Apply to Beastborne:** when the player earns something, the HUD should react somewhere. Could be:
- The player level badge pulses
- A reaction icon briefly appears next to the currency that grew
- The notification badge flashes a color matching the reward tier

Not required for every reward, but for milestone+ moments it adds a layer of "the game is paying attention to me."

### Relic acquisition formality
Picking up a relic (permanent upgrade) is more ceremonial than picking up a boon. Longer sequence, music shift, different sound vocabulary. The game teaches you through *duration* which things are bigger deals.

**Apply to Beastborne:** tier the juice duration by reward importance. A routine mission claim is ~300ms. A weekly reward is ~800ms. A milestone is ~1500ms. Day 7 legendary is ~4-6 seconds. Player learns implicitly "longer animation = bigger deal" without needing to read anything.

### The "held moment"
Between stages of a reveal, Hades *holds* — a frame where nothing animates but sound is playing and anticipation is building. These held moments are load-bearing. Without them, the reveal feels rushed.

**Apply to Beastborne:** when designing a reveal, build in hold frames. Don't chain animations back-to-back. A 200ms pause at the peak of a reveal sells the weight better than another 200ms of motion.

## What NOT to borrow

- **The isometric action-RPG framing.** Hades is a different genre. Don't try to adapt Zagreus's combat feedback to Beastborne's battle system — they work because of the camera/pacing/genre differences.
- **The voice acting budget.** Hades gets massive impact from Supergiant's voice work. Beastborne shouldn't pretend to have that. Use sound and visual pacing to carry the weight instead.
- **Greek pantheon aesthetic.** Beastborne has its own mythology-rooted world. Don't import Hades's specific look; import its *pacing*.

## Pairing note

Hades + HSR is the combo for Day 7. HSR teaches you the single-item pickup grammar (light pillar, silhouette, name slam), Hades teaches you the pacing and earned-feeling. Use HSR's aesthetics inside Hades's structure, and strip out the gacha baggage from both.
